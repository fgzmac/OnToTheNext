## Application interaction and identity

Existing recommendation Sources/details and saved-item details contain a collapsed Google section. Each request is explicit: find/correct match, link a reviewed candidate, context, reviews, or one photo. No Google work is scheduled on render, startup, Day switch, Add or polling. Saved item reopening uses the application recommendation relationship and permitted place ID; refreshing context is explicit. Independent names, descriptions, durations, rankings, decisions, ordering, progress, reservations and snapshots stay application-owned. Research overlays and events are deliberately ineligible in this first slice.

Exact normalized name/approved independent aliases, Japan country, city and corroborating area/address (or a specific official website when independent area data is absent) are required. A website cannot override a conflicting or uncorroborated known area. Supplied conflicting operator hosts or specific competing Pokémon/teamLab branch URLs are rejected. Unknown legitimate aliases can therefore remain unmatched and require independent source review; no live accuracy is claimed. No first-result acceptance, name-only acceptance or automatic relocation. Closed/unknown business status and moved IDs are rejected. Multiple corroborated candidates are explicitly ambiguous. Organizer confirmation is a signed, five-minute, trip/application-identity-bound token. Unknown names/branches remain unmatched; do not weaken matching based on a synthetic success. Neighborhood context is only an area anchor, never verification of an app-authored walk or supplier tour. Google venue data never verifies an event occurrence, admission or tickets.
