## Fourth experiment

Validate whether the mobile Today experience is fast, clear, and useful during a realistic moving-around day.

### Test scenario
Use a San Jose test day with:
- one fixed-time reservation,
- one flexible activity,
- one intentional free-time block,
- one return-to-home/hotel-style break,
- one nearby-discovery decision,
- one shared expense,
- one simulated 30-minute delay.

### Tester tasks
1. Identify the next activity.
2. Determine when to leave.
3. Find reservation/ticket details.
4. Understand the remaining day.
5. Use the free-time block to find something nearby.
6. Return to the home/hotel equivalent.
7. Add an expense.
8. Recover after a simulated delay.

### Success target
The tester can complete the day without hunting through multiple screens for basic trip-state information.

External tools are acceptable for:
- turn-by-turn navigation,
- payment,
- official ticket purchase.

The app should remain the source of truth for:
- what happens next,
- timing,
- reservation context,
- free-time choices,
- and the day plan.
