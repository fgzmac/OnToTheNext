## Fifth experiment

Validate whether the themed Share Trip experience is genuinely useful and exciting enough to justify its V1 design effort.

### Variants under test

1. **Plain**
   - simple itinerary list
   - app link

2. **Polished**
   - destination-themed cover
   - clean city/day highlights
   - hotels and major reservations
   - app link

3. **Rich**
   - stronger visual treatment
   - photography/art direction
   - map/route summary
   - more visual day-by-day presentation
   - app link

### Tester questions
- Which version would you actually send?
- Which is easiest to understand?
- Which feels most exciting to receive?
- Is any version too busy?
- Is anything important missing?
- Would you prefer text/email/link over a downloadable file?

### Success target
The preferred themed version should feel noticeably more useful/exciting than the plain itinerary without becoming harder to read.

### Outcome
Use results to decide how much visual/theming complexity belongs in V1.
