## First experiment

Validate whether small batches of factual recommendations genuinely save research effort and produce itinerary-worthy choices.

### Test format
1. Use the Japan pilot.
2. Present a small batch of factual recommendations.
3. User accepts or denies each option.
4. Use those decisions to refine the next batch.
5. Repeat for several rounds.
6. Observe whether enough accepted options emerge to shape a useful itinerary.

### What to measure
- acceptance rate,
- whether later batches improve,
- whether accepted options are itinerary-worthy,
- whether the user still feels forced to leave the app and research independently,
- whether recommendation cards contain enough information to make a decision.

### Success signal
The app meaningfully reduces research effort and produces enough accepted options to help form the trip.

### Failure signal
The user repeatedly rejects suggestions, finds them generic, or still needs substantial outside research before deciding.
