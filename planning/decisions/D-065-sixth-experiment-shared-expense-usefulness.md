## Sixth experiment

Validate whether shared expense tracking belongs naturally inside the travel experience without making the product feel finance-heavy.

### Test scenario
Use a two-person simulated trip day with:
- shared hotel cost,
- one meal,
- one activity,
- one transit expense,
- one expense paid by each traveler,
- one uneven split,
- one final settlement.

### Tasks
Ask both travelers to:
1. Add expenses they paid.
2. Check who currently owes whom.
3. Correct one expense.
4. Review planned versus actual spend.
5. Record a final settlement.

### Success target
Both travelers can explain the final balance without:
- a spreadsheet,
- manual calculation,
- or a separate expense app.

At the same time:
- Home should not become finance-heavy,
- Today should keep expense entry quick,
- expense tracking should remain a supporting trip feature rather than the center of the product.
