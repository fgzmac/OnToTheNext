# Research — Core-city hotel shortlist

**Status:** PROPOSED research, not a decision or booking.  
**Updated:** 2026-09-20.  
**Purpose:** Maintain five meaningful hotel candidates per core city against the confirmed trip preferences and approved city schedule. Exact personal dates and financial amounts stay out of this public file.

## Evaluation criteria

- Upscale/comfortable feel
- Convenient for the approved itinerary
- One large bed available
- Value relative to the owner's accepted personal search reference
- Useful amenities
- Strong transit access
- Practical for hotel breaks and casual exploration
- Community feedback used for lived-experience context
- Official/property evidence used for material room/amenity facts

## Tokyo — Ginza / Tsukiji cluster

1. **Mitsui Garden Hotel Ginza Tsukiji — best amenities / strong overall**
   - Queen and Wide King room categories.
   - Washer/dryer and microwave in every room.
   - Large public bath (not represented as a natural hot spring).
   - Strong Ginza/Tsukiji access.
   - Tradeoff: some rooms are compact; nearby construction/noise should be checked.

2. **Daiwa Roynet Hotel Ginza PREMIER — strong value/convenience**
   - One-bed Superior/King Double categories.
   - Semi-separated bathrooms in most rooms.
   - 24-hour coin laundry.
   - Very strong Ginza-itchome/Ginza/Higashi-Ginza transit access.
   - Tradeoff: laundry is shared unless a larger special room is chosen.

3. **Millennium Mitsui Garden Hotel Tokyo — best transit/shopping convenience**
   - Exceptional Higashi-Ginza/Ginza station access.
   - Strong fit for Ginza shopping and returning to the hotel between activities.
   - Polished modern rooms and strong bathroom layout.
   - Tradeoff: rooms can be compact; fewer major hotel facilities.

4. **Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Ginza — quieter polished alternative**
   - Central Ginza with strong shopping/dining/transit access.
   - Frequently described as quiet for the neighborhood.
   - Useful logistics/luggage-forwarding support.
   - Tradeoff: room/bathroom quality varies by category; less amenity-rich than the top choice.

5. **Hotel Musse Ginza Meitetsu — value-oriented Ginza alternate**
   - King room categories with 180cm beds; separated bath/toilet layouts in most rooms.
   - Coin laundry.
   - Ginza/Shimbashi/Higashi-Ginza access.
   - Tradeoff: basic rooms can be very small; nearby demolition/construction is scheduled through 2028 and should be considered for daytime rest.

## Kyoto — central / Higashiyama alternatives

1. **Hilton Garden Inn Kyoto Shijo Karasuma — best balanced base**
   - Central, useful for the owner's spread-out Kyoto itinerary.
   - King room categories.
   - Laundry, fitness, restaurants nearby.
   - Tradeoff: not directly beside the most atmospheric east-Kyoto areas; some days still require transit.

2. **GOOD NATURE HOTEL KYOTO — best upscale downtown experience**
   - Extremely central near Kyoto Kawaramachi, Gion, shopping, Nishiki/Pontocho.
   - Larger rooms and strong on-site dining/wellness.
   - Tradeoff: may price above the preferred range; some central-facing rooms can have street noise.

3. **Cross Hotel Kyoto — best food/nightlife/walkability balance**
   - Central Sanjo/Kawaramachi location, walkable to Pontocho, shopping, parts of Gion.
   - Standard King room category and separated bath/toilet layout.
   - Tradeoff: no coin laundry; paid front-desk laundry instead.

4. **Hotel Resol Trinity Kyoto — best public-bath / quieter central option**
   - Double room with one 160cm bed.
   - Large guest public bath, coin laundry, matcha set.
   - Walkable to Teramachi/Shinkyogoku/Kawaramachi/Pontocho.
   - Tradeoff: less directly positioned for Kyoto Station and some southern/western itinerary anchors.

5. **NOHGA HOTEL KIYOMIZU KYOTO — best design / Higashiyama atmosphere**
   - King rooms with 180cm beds.
   - 24-hour coin laundry, gym, bakery/restaurant/rooftop bar.
   - Strong for eastern Kyoto/Gion/Kiyomizu atmosphere.
   - Tradeoff: less central for casual returns from Arashiyama/Nijo/downtown; King/Queen rooms are shower-only.

## Osaka — Shinsaibashi / Namba cluster

1. **HOTEL THE FLAG Shinsaibashi — best overall fit**
   - Double rooms with one 170cm bed.
   - Excellent Shinsaibashi shopping/Dotonbori access.
   - Stylish laundry room and shared spaces.
   - Tradeoff: lively neighborhood can mean occasional noise; standard rooms are compact.

2. **HOTEL ROYAL CLASSIC OSAKA — best station convenience / upscale**
   - Direct connection to Namba Station.
   - Standard Double rooms with one 180cm bed, 25.6–31m².
   - Very easy Dotonbori/Namba access and luggage logistics.
   - Tradeoff: published standard pricing is premium; no fitness room noted.

3. **Citadines Namba Osaka — best space / extended-stay amenities**
   - Double room types, larger apartment-style categories, kitchens/washer-dryers in some units plus coin laundry.
   - Walkable to Kuromon, Dotonbori, Namba and Shinsekai direction.
   - Tradeoff: approximately 10-minute walk to Namba Station rather than direct station access.

4. **Cross Hotel Osaka — best Dotonbori proximity**
   - About 1 minute from Dotonbori and 3 minutes from Namba subway.
   - Double categories including 180cm-bed Deluxe Double.
   - Excellent for casual evening exploration without transit.
   - Tradeoff: busiest nightlife/tourist core may be less restful than a quieter base.

5. **Hotel Nikko Osaka — best direct Shinsaibashi transit**
   - Direct connection to Shinsaibashi Station.
   - Walkable to Dotonbori/Americamura and convenient for USJ/Kansai travel.
   - Full-service hotel positioning with laundry/room service.
   - Tradeoff: more conventional large-hotel feel; verify exact one-bed room category and date-specific rate before ranking above boutique alternatives.

## Ranking principles

Do not choose from star rating alone. Rank against:
- the actual itinerary,
- bed configuration,
- desired hotel-break behavior,
- casual evening exploration,
- amenity priorities,
- full stay cost,
- cancellation terms,
- and date-specific room availability.

For the product, show 4–5 meaningful choices when inventory supports it; do not pad weak options.
