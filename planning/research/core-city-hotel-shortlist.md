# Research — Core-city hotel shortlist

**Status:** PROPOSED research, not a decision or booking.  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-20.  
**Purpose:** Compare hotels against confirmed trip preferences and the approved city schedule without publishing private dates or personal budget amounts.

## Evaluation criteria

- Upscale/comfortable feel
- Convenient location for the approved Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka plan
- One large bed available
- Value relative to the owner's accepted search reference
- Useful amenities where relevant
- Strong transit access
- Quiet/comfortable enough for hotel breaks
- Community feedback considered alongside official property information

## Tokyo — initial candidates

1. **Mitsui Garden Hotel Ginza Tsukiji**
   - Strong fit for Ginza/Tsukiji access.
   - Official property information says all rooms have washer-dryers and microwaves.
   - One-bed Queen and Wide King room categories exist.
   - Strong example of amenity-aware ranking.

2. **Daiwa Roynet Hotel Ginza PREMIER**
   - Ginza location with multiple nearby stations.
   - Official room list includes a King Double room.
   - 24-hour coin laundry on site.
   - Strong value/comfort comparison candidate.

3. **Mitsui Garden Hotel Ginza-gochome**
   - Excellent Higashi-Ginza access and central location.
   - Keep as an alternate pending room/rate comparison.

## Kyoto — initial candidates

1. **Hilton Garden Inn Kyoto Shijo Karasuma**
   - Central, transit-connected, close to restaurants/shops.
   - Community discussions often favor the Shijo/Karasuma area for convenience.
   - Strong baseline for the owner's balanced itinerary.

2. **GOOD NATURE HOTEL KYOTO**
   - Very strong location near Kyoto Kawaramachi and Gion.
   - Hotel offers laundry and extensive food options.
   - Current forward pricing signals may place some dates above the accepted reference, so treat as an upgrade comparison rather than assume fit.

3. **NOHGA HOTEL KIYOMIZU KYOTO**
   - King room categories available.
   - Coin laundry and guest laundry service.
   - More atmospheric/cultural feel, but location tradeoff versus the central Shijo/Karasuma base should be made explicit.

## Osaka — initial candidates

1. **HOTEL THE FLAG Shinsaibashi**
   - Excellent Shinsaibashi position, near shopping and nightlife.
   - Standard/Deluxe Double rooms use one 170cm bed.
   - Stylish laundry room.
   - Strong comfort/location/value fit.

2. **HOTEL ROYAL CLASSIC OSAKA**
   - Direct Namba-station connection.
   - Standard Double rooms use one 180cm bed and are relatively spacious.
   - Strong convenience/upscale comparison; verify date-specific pricing.

3. **Citadines Namba Osaka**
   - Larger-room/aparthotel style and quieter immediate setting according to community feedback.
   - Good option if space/extended-stay amenities outrank being directly in Shinsaibashi's busiest core.

## Source-use principle

Official hotel pages verify room/amenity facts. Community discussions help assess neighborhood feel, quietness, convenience, and traveler experience. Neither source type alone establishes live availability or final price.
