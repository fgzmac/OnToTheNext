## High-priority actions

### Universal Studios Japan / SUPER NINTENDO WORLD — planned December 3
- Official USJ guidance: tickets generally go on sale two months before the visit date, with exceptions by ticket type.
- For December 3, begin purchase/checking on approximately October 3.
- Universal Express Passes are limited inventory and generally sold from about two months before the visit.
- Some Express Passes include guaranteed SUPER NINTENDO WORLD area admission.
- SUPER NINTENDO WORLD may require advance-booking, timed-entry, or standby-entry access.
- Since January 5, 2026, same-day timed/standby area tickets require the official USJ app.
- Studio Pass and Express Pass are separate products.

**Recommended owner action:** Treat early October as a must-check window. Compare Studio Pass + a Nintendo-inclusive Express Pass rather than relying solely on same-day entry.

### SHIBUYA SKY — planned November 27
- Official site sells entry tickets through two weeks ahead.
- For November 27, the two-week window begins November 13.
- Web sold-out adult tickets are also unavailable at the walk-up counter.
- Afternoon/evening pricing differs from earlier slots.

**Recommended owner action:** Target a sunset/night admission and check immediately when the date opens.

### teamLab Planets — planned December 6
- Official individual ticket store uses timed admission.
- Official group-ticket page says December tickets will become available starting in late September.
- Exact individual-ticket release timing was not confirmed from the public page in this research.

**Recommended owner action:** Start checking official December individual inventory in late September and book once the desired date/time becomes available.

## Medium / optional reservation items

### Nijo-jo — planned November 29
- Ninomaru Palace does not require reservations.
- Honmaru Palace requires advance timed reservation.
- Ninomaru Palace is closed every Tuesday in December; the planned Sunday date avoids that issue.
- Full castle opening hours and closure rules should be rechecked shortly before travel.

**Recommended owner action:** Decide whether Honmaru Palace is important. If not, ordinary Ninomaru visit does not create a hard booking deadline.

### Ippodo Kyoto Main Store / Kaboku Tearoom — candidate December 1
- Hands-on brewing in Kaboku Tearoom requires no reservation and reservations are not accepted.
- A separate tea tasting experience does require reservation, at least four days in advance through Ippodo's contact form.
- The store/tearoom is normally closed on the second Wednesday of each month; the planned Tuesday does not conflict with that rule.

**Recommended owner action:** Keep hands-on brewing flexible unless the formal tasting is preferred.

### Solaniwa Onsen — candidate December 4
- Official site shows opening hours 11:00–23:00 and December 9 as the December 2026 closed day; December 4 is not listed as a closed day.
- Advance tickets are sold.
- Private natural-hot-spring baths are available at extra cost.
- Mixed-gender private-bath use is limited to family relationships under the facility's rule, including spouses; do not assume an unmarried couple qualifies.
- Normal public bathing is separate by gender.

**Recommended owner action:** Keep standard admission flexible; only treat private bath as a reservation item after confirming eligibility/preferences.
