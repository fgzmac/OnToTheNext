## Current direction and boundaries

**D-026 two workstreams:** Actual Japan planning and reservation preparation now; phone-first spontaneous discovery tested in San Jose as the product develops. Preparing the real plan may use research/manual work before the app exists. Desktop parity is not the initial priority; no OS, native/web, provider, simulator, hosting, or app-store choice follows. No booking transaction, payment, automatic notification, or location access is authorized.

**D-025 history:** Early San Jose use → working beta → Japan-focused simulations was the original software-testing sequence. The beta prerequisite must not delay real Japan itinerary or reservation research under D-026. Later software rehearsals may still follow beta. Local success does not prove Japan coverage. Controlled scenarios, historical examples, and current independently checked information need separate evidence. Simulating a date does not establish future events, availability, or prices. Geographic local testing is not localhost-only development, and San Jose is a public test-area label, not a home address or tracking permission.

**D-024 effort and day example:** Owner effort is as necessary, not an invented full-time schedule or unlimited capacity. Example: one/two main experiences → free time → hotel rest → user reopens app → radius-based discovery → optional outing. This is not a daily quota, mandatory return, compulsory outing, fixed break, selected radius, GPS permission, fatigue detection, or automatic plan change. Staying at the hotel is valid. Nearby distance and feasible travel/entry are separate checks.

**D-023 problems:** Choosing worthwhile experiences and their order, organizing coherently, balancing effort with shopping/food/free time, finding events during travel, and getting started amid hotel/travel/quality variables. Proposed responses include a manageable starting path, hotel-informed ordering, explicit unknowns, day-balance checks, and preserved flexible windows. No nearest-first objective, required hotel before discovery, health inference, energy score, fixed ratio, or automatic draft mode selected.

**D-018 standards:** Suggestion quality, useful current nearby discovery, then seamless operation. Same-day is first-version work, not later-only. Sharing stays; correctness, privacy, reliability, and accessibility remain necessary. Coverage, freshness, response times, result counts, ranking, and quantitative tests remain open.

**D-019 taste:** Scenic participatory/local combinations appeal; supplied standalone novelty/statue examples do not. Not a global history ban, companion taste, ability/risk/luxury assumption, or verified trip option.

**D-020 formats:** Both operator excursions and app combinations. An assembled sequence is not one provider package/price/reservation. Shorter/all-day duration idea remains tentative; no default buckets. Check total time and included transfers; do not invent shortened fixed tours.

**D-021 sources:** Real experiences, positive Reddit posts, strong reviews, date calendars, and destination highlights. No mandatory popularity, thresholds/counts, API/scraper, account histories, paid access, copying/training rights, or marketing-channel selection. Preserve relevant criticisms, duplicates/conflicts, and occurrence dates. Appeal evidence and current logistics are distinct.

**D-022 local/cultural:** Both included. Local recommendation origin differs from proximity; culture concerns context/experience/learning. Categories/source verification open. No marketplace, hosts, partnerships, fabricated local consensus/authenticity, restricted access, or guaranteed availability.

**Timing:** November 10, 2026 remains the software-test target, not the date to start Japan planning or a booking deadline. Private travel values already supplied; no need to request them again. Tokyo included, not necessarily exclusive. Airports, transfers, stays, and other cities still need actual inputs.

**Travel budget:** Per-person range excluding airfare, not app funding or payment authority. Currency/categories/total-versus-remaining open. No forced minimum, firm maximum, equal split, or conversion chosen.

**App policy:** Free first; flexible $100/$150 references, not spend targets or cutoffs. Review purchases individually. Greater discretionary investment follows owner acceptance. Tools, one-time costs, licenses separate; re-verify previous price references before use.

**Reservations:** Requirement, booking status, and payment differ. Praise, calendar entries, and proximity do not secure tickets or tables. Preparing a release/action list is not automatic reminder scheduling or authority to transact. No blanket guarantee that all options require months of lead time or are bookable now.

Do not silently remove confirmed capabilities to remain free. Prepared data tests interactions, not current-world quality. Surface quality, scope, time, and cost conflicts explicitly.
