## Current direction and boundaries

**D-029 lodging preference:** Separate hotel stay in each main city for this trip. The app should ask each traveler about separate city stays versus fewer bases rather than hardcode the owner's preference. Exact wording, placement, defaults, optional comparison answer, persistence, and change previews are still proposed. No property, nightly budget, city order, or nights selected. A return to a city need not mean a different hotel, and an optional day trip does not require another hotel. Proposed scheduling checks include transfers, packing/luggage, check-in/out, activity time, and rest; no automatic modification or booking is authorized.

**D-028 sequencing and baseline:** Core main-city itinerary and booking needs first; optional destinations afterward. Only flights booked, as reported. Positive interest in earlier candidates does not select them. Completion criteria, exact substeps, and hotel-versus-activity order remain open. Do not require all purchases upfront, invent confirmations, or automatically modify/cancel commitments when evaluating an extension. The extra-destination capability remains included rather than removed or relegated to an unspecified future release.

**D-027 destinations:** Starting cities supplied; further cities recommended because of experiences worth the trip, not obscurity alone. Proposed explanation includes appeal, dated/seasonal relevance, day trip versus overnight, additional travel/lodging/cost, displaced time, reservations, and evidence. D-028 now controls sequencing. No algorithm, extra-city quota, hotel limit, or automatic rewrite selected. Candidate research is not a booking or working app feature.

**D-026 workstreams:** Japan research/manual planning now; phone-first spontaneous discovery tested in San Jose as the product develops. Desktop parity is not priority. No OS, native/web, provider, simulator, host, store publication, transaction, notification, or location access follows.

**D-025 history:** Local early use → beta → Japan simulations was the earlier test sequence. It cannot delay actual Japan preparation. Software rehearsals may still follow beta. San Jose success is not Japan coverage. Separate controlled/historical examples from current checked data; simulated dates do not establish future availability. Local geographic testing is not localhost-only hosting; San Jose is a test-area label, not a residence record.

**D-024 example:** One/two main experiences → free time/hotel rest → user opens app → radius discovery → optional outing. No daily quota, mandatory return, compulsory outing, break length, radius/default, GPS consent, fatigue detection, or automatic changes selected. Staying in is valid. Distance and feasible travel/entry differ. As-needed effort does not imply unlimited capacity.

**D-023 problems:** Choice/order, coherent planning, energy versus shopping/food/free time, dated events, and starting amid hotel/travel/quality variables. Proposed manageable steps, known-hotel context, explicit unknowns, preserved free time. No nearest-first objective, compulsory hotel before exploration, health inference, energy score, fixed ratio, or automatic-draft design approved.

**D-018 standards:** Quality, same-day usefulness, seamless function. Basic same-day stays first-version work; sharing remains. Correctness, privacy, reliability, accessibility not waived. Exact coverage/freshness/latency/counts/weights/tests open.

**D-019 taste:** Scenic, participatory, locally distinctive combinations appeal more than supplied standalone novelty/statue examples. Not a global history ban, companion agreement, ability/risk/luxury assumption, or verified trip option. D-027 does not make every unfamiliar city appealing by default.

**D-020 formats:** Both operator excursions and app combinations. Separate components are not automatically one package/price/reservation. Shorter/all-day categories tentative. Check total time/included transfers; do not invent shortened fixed tours.

**D-021 sources:** Firsthand accounts, positive Reddit posts, reviews, calendars, destination highlights. No mandatory popularity, thresholds/counts, API/scraper, account history, licensing, copying/training rights, or marketing channel. Preserve critical caveats, duplicates/conflicts, occurrence dates; appeal and current logistics differ.

**D-022 local/cultural:** Included. Source origin differs from geographic proximity; cultural context differs from one activity category. No marketplace, hosts, partnerships, fabricated consensus/authenticity, restricted access, or guaranteed entry. Categories/verification open.

**Timing and budget:** November 10, 2026 is software testing, not the beginning of travel planning or a booking deadline. Exact travel inputs are supplied privately; starting cities, hotel-base choice, and broad booking status now answered. Trip range is per person excluding airfare, not app funding or payment authority; currency/categories/remaining-total meaning open. No forced minimum, hard cap, equal split, or conversion selected. App free-first $100/$150 references are flexible; review purchases separately. Tools/setup/licenses remain outside the runtime reference.

**Reservations and evidence:** Requirement, booking, payment differ. Praise or a calendar entry does not secure admission. An action list is not a scheduled reminder or authority to transact. No universal lead time, current inventory, or working beta claimed. Do not silently drop features or use fictional data as real evidence to stay free.
