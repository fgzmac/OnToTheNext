## Current direction and safeguards

**Latest records:** [D-026](decisions/D-026-phone-first-and-japan-priority.md), [D-027](decisions/D-027-core-cities-and-worthwhile-detours.md), [D-028](decisions/D-028-main-cities-first-and-booking-baseline.md), [D-029](decisions/D-029-per-city-hotels-and-stay-preference.md), [D-030](decisions/D-030-recommendation-led-planning-and-hotel-value.md), [D-031](decisions/D-031-hotel-search-reference-approved.md), [D-032](decisions/D-032-core-city-schedule-approved.md). Earlier reasoning remains in [the decision register](decisions.md).

- **Guided planning:** Supply worthwhile options, explain differences, and refine from reactions while preserving accepted decisions. Exact controls, counts, learning, and persistence remain open. Custom input/correction/stopping are proposed safeguards; no endless upselling, unsolicited notifications, auto-booking, or automatic filling of free time.
- **Hotel preferences:** Separate main-city stays; upscale, comfortable, convenient, value-conscious; no hostels for this traveler, not a global ban. Accepted USD per-room nightly search reference is not minimum spend, unlimited upgrade authority, or verified inventory. Two-adult room comparison does not decide bedding, exact room, or equal expense splitting. Evaluate actual room, full cost, taxes/fees, inclusions, cancellation, and activity access. No star, size, amenity, quietness, or walk-time hard threshold selected.
- **Main-city-first:** Approved schedule is the baseline. Optional worthwhile destinations come after the core itinerary, not an unspecified later release. Show added travel, nights, cost, displaced choices, and booking consequences; no automatic rewrite or detour quota. Schedule review, booking confirmation, and payment are different.
- **Two workstreams:** Actual Japan preparation now, phone-first spontaneous-discovery tests in San Jose. Former local→beta→Japan simulations cannot block the trip. Local success does not prove Japan coverage. Controlled/historical examples and current independently checked information are different evidence. Local testing does not select localhost hosting, native/web, GPS spoofing, or residence disclosure.
- **Day balance:** Main experience(s), optional hotel rest, then user-initiated nearby search is an example, not a quota, mandatory return, fixed break, or obligation to go out. Radius is not travel-time/entry feasibility. No health/fatigue inference, compulsory hotel booking before discovery, nearest-first objective, fixed energy score, background tracking, or automatic completion detection.
- **Standards:** Quality first, same-day usefulness second, seamless functionality third. Same-day remains first-version work; sharing remains. Correctness/privacy/reliability/accessibility are necessary. Weights, coverage, freshness, latency, result counts, and numerical tests remain open.
- **Preferences/formats/sources:** Scenic participatory/local combinations appeal to the organizer, not all users or automatically the companion. No global history ban or physical/risk assumption. Offer real operator excursions and app-assembled sequences without inventing one combined package/price/booking. Short/all-day categories remain tentative. Firsthand/Reddit/review/calendar/highlight/local/cultural inputs require evidence and access evaluation; positive opinions do not prove logistics. No fabricated source labels, authenticity, local consensus, or restricted cultural access. No platform, scraping/license/retention/copying/training permission, marketplace, or partnership selected. Preserve critical caveats, duplicate/conflict handling, and occurrence-date distinctions.
- **Resources:** November 10, 2026 remains a software-test target, not a booking deadline or delivery guarantee. Flexible effort is not unlimited capacity. Free-first app costs and flexible USD 100/150 references do not authorize purchases; tools/setup/licenses are separate. Re-verify current prices and terms when relevant. Per-person trip range excluding airfare is distinct from app costs and hotel room rates. Currency/categories/total-versus-remaining/splitting remain partly open.
- **Authority/evidence:** No booking, payment, cancellation, notification, monitoring, implementation, selected stack/AI/provider, or accepted working product follows from research or a planning approval. Do not silently drop capabilities or misrepresent simulated data to stay free. Surface scope, cost, quality, and timing conflicts.
