## Current direction and boundaries

**D-030 recommendation-led planning:** Use known context to suggest useful choices; let reactions refine subsequent suggestions while preserving accepted decisions. Custom entries, corrections, and stopping remain important proposed controls. Exact numbers, weights, algorithms, AI/providers, and data persistence open. Do not equate continually helpful with unsolicited notifications, tracking, auto-filled free time, endless choices, or booking transactions. Main-city-first still applies.

**D-030 hotel preferences and comparison:** Upscale, comfortable, convenient, value-conscious, no hostels for the initial traveler. Not a global hostel ban or an approved star rating, private-bathroom/room-size/amenity list, neighborhood, cap, or property. Evaluate offered-room quality and location relative to the trip. Show budget points and total-stay tradeoffs rather than only asking a number. Proposed USD 150/200/250/300/400 nightly room scenarios and USD 200–260 search reference are not quotes or chosen limits. Label currency, charge basis, occupancy, taxes/mandatory fees, inclusions, and cancellation conditions. A cheaper room that fits should not be excluded to meet an artificial minimum. Equal sharing is illustrative only; the app budget is unrelated.

**D-029 hotel bases:** Separate stay in each main city for this trip. Ask travelers rather than hardcoding that answer. Exact wording, placement, defaults, compare-both option, persistence, and previews open. A return to a city need not mean a different property; a day trip need not mean a hotel change. Consider luggage/transfers/check-in/out/activity time/rest; no booking modification authorized.

**D-028 sequence/baseline:** Main-city itinerary and booking needs first, extensions afterward. Only flights booked, user-reported. Interest in candidates is not selection. Completion/substep order open. No requirement to purchase everything before optional discovery or automatic changes/cancellations. Extension capability remains included.

**D-027 destinations:** Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka starting cities; other cities recommended for worthwhile experiences, not obscurity alone. Proposed support explains appeal, dates, day trip/overnight, added travel/lodging/cost, displaced time, reservations, evidence. No quota, hotel limit, algorithm, or automatic rewrite. Research is not an app feature or booking.

**D-026 workstreams:** Japan manual research/planning now; phone-first spontaneous discovery tested in San Jose. No OS/native/web/provider/simulator/host/store/transaction/notification/location-access choice. Desktop parity is not priority.

**D-025 history:** Local→beta→Japan simulations is not a prerequisite for actual trip preparation. Rehearsals may follow beta. Local success is not Japan coverage; controlled/historical scenarios and current verified information differ. Local testing is not localhost-only; San Jose is a test-area label, not residence/tracking permission.

**D-024 flow/effort:** One/two main experiences → free time/hotel rest → user opens app → optional radius discovery. Not a mandatory quota, hotel return, extra outing, break, radius, GPS permission, fatigue detection, or automatic changes. Staying in is valid. Distance and usable travel/entry differ. As-needed effort is not unlimited capacity.

**D-023 problems:** Choice/order, coherent organization, energy versus shopping/food/free time, dated events, starting amid hotel/travel/quality variables. Proposed manageable steps, known-hotel context, explicit unknowns, preserved free time. No distance-only objective, required booking before discovery, health inference, energy score, or fixed ratio. D-030 now confirms guided idea refinement, not a particular automatic-draft mechanism.

**D-018 standards:** Quality, same-day usefulness, seamless function. Same-day remains first-version work; sharing retained. Correctness/privacy/reliability/accessibility not waived. Coverage/freshness/latency/counts/weights/tests open.

**D-019 taste:** Scenic participatory/local combinations appeal more than the supplied standalone novelty/statue examples. Not a universal history ban, companion agreement, physical/risk/luxury assumption, or verified trip option.

**D-020 formats:** Operator excursions and app combinations. Separate components do not imply one package/price/reservation. Shorter/all-day categories tentative. Check total time/included transfers; do not invent shorter fixed tours.

**D-021 sources:** Firsthand/Reddit/reviews/calendars/highlights. No mandatory popularity, thresholds, APIs/scraping/history access/licenses/copying/training rights or marketing channel. Preserve critical caveats, duplicates/conflicts, actual occurrences. Appeal evidence is not current logistics.

**D-022 local/cultural:** Both included; source origin differs from proximity. Categories and verification open; no marketplace/hosts/partnerships/invented authenticity/local consensus/restricted access/guaranteed entry.

**Timing/budgets:** November 10, 2026 is software testing, not a booking deadline or start of trip planning. Private travel values are supplied. Trip budget is per person excluding airfare; currency/categories/total/remaining open. No forced minimum, hard cap, split rule, or conversion. Free-first app references are flexible; review purchases specifically. Tools/setup/licenses are separate.

**Reservations/evidence:** Requirement, booked, and paid differ. Praise/calendar listings do not secure entry. An action list is not a reminder or payment authorization. No universal lead time, inventory, or working beta claimed. Do not drop features or fabricate evidence to remain free.
